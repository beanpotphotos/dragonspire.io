You've tried the chatbot DM. So have we.

You asked it to run a dungeon. It forgot the goblin's hit points, rolled a 23 on a d20, renamed the innkeeper twice, and handed you the dragon's hoard because you asked nicely. A language model on its own is a storyteller with no memory and no rulebook. We made something different.

DragonSpire gives the storyteller a whole table to work with.

  • Real dice, real rules.

    Fantasy Grounds' ruleset rolls every attack and applies every hit. The AI reads the result; it cannot make one up.

  • A world that remembers.

    Places, people and quests live in a database, not in the AI's context memory. They get handed to the AI game master every turn for consistency.

  • Fair by design.

    You roll for your character. The AI never decides your action, you click it, type it or say it. The GM says no when you try to pocket loot nobody offered. It can check your inventory to see if you have a Wand of Fireballs.

How it works

Five parts. One game master.

Each part does the job it is good at.

  • Cory

    The brain

    AI

    Decides what happens next and writes what the game master says.

  • Dee Emma

    The voice

    AI

    Hears each player in Discord / text chat and answers aloud in a narrator voice

  • FGU Link

    The hands

    Code, no AI

    Works the Fantasy Grounds table: dice, tokens, maps, the combat tracker. Never calls an AI.

  • Campaign Manager

    The memory

    Database

    Every place, NPC and quest in your world, written by your GM and read by the game master every turn.

  • DragonLibrary

    The rulebook

    Database

    D&D 2024 rules and thousands of Fantasy Grounds effect codes, matched to your table and looked up, never guessed.

What happens when you say “I swing my axe at the goblin.”

  1. You speak.

    Dee Emma hears you in Discord.

  2. Cory listens.

    Was that meant for the game master? Is it your turn? It checks Fantasy Grounds.

  3. Cory picks a move.

    The AI asks for an attack roll; it does not invent one.

  4. Fantasy Grounds rolls.

    The real 5E ruleset rolls and applies the damage.

  5. A receipt comes back.

    In about a tenth of a second, with what actually happened.

  6. Dee Emma tells the story.

Three tables. One game master.

  • Fantasy Grounds Unity.

    It reads the live combat tracker, sheets and maps, and acts with the ruleset's own functions.

  • Discord voice.

    A narrator voice and the manners to wait for a pause.

  • The web.

    Chat with the GM, roll 3D dice, see your sheet, the party and the initiative order. You can even play without Fantasy Grounds running.

Meet Dee Emma. She has table manners.

Dee Emma is DragonSpire's voice interface on Discord, and its player-facing interface on the web. She hears each player and answers in a narrator voice.

  • Waits for a pause before she speaks.
  • Stops when you talk over her.
  • Ignores side chatter that is not for the GM.
  • Drops a line that went stale while it waited.
  • Nudges a quiet table once, gently, and only the player whose turn it is.

For game masters: if you want a co-GM, not a replacement.

Pick how much DragonSpire does. In Assistant mode it drafts and you approve each move on a card. Monster actions are suggestions until you confirm.

  1. Passive

    acts only when you ask

  2. Assistant

    drafts, you approve

  3. Autonomous

    runs the table; still being proven
