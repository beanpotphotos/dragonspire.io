The engraved schematic

One visual language for DragonSpire.io and dragonspireinteractive.com: the blueprint of a wizard's tower, drawn by an engineer. Fine gold linework on night-blue ink, one colour per part of the product.

Colour: one per part

The same part always wears the same colour, in every diagram, on both sites. Large chip is dark mode, small chip is light mode.

Motion and voice

The rules every page follows.

Slow and purposeful

Data moves as small glowing dots along gold lines. Diagrams draw themselves once when scrolled into view. No bouncing, no heavy parallax.

Still when asked

With reduced motion turned on, every diagram shows its finished state, fully drawn, with no moving dots.

Plain and specific

Short sentences. Real numbers instead of adjectives. Slightly wry about chatbot DMs, never smug.

Never: purple-to-blue gradients · robot or brain icons · chat bubbles as the main visual · dragon clip art · parchment and candles · emoji · neon green · revolutionary · magic · unleash
